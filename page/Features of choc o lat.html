Introduction

Chocolate is a famous snack product enjoyed worldwide. It was originally made from cocoa beans, and over time, milk chocolate was introduced by adding sugar, giving it a sweeter taste. Since then, it has been sold in supermarkets and shopping centers for people to enjoy. Although rich in carbohydrates and a source of quick energy, chocolate also contains caffeine and theobromine, which can be harmful to dogs. Most chocolate production occurs in European countries such as Germany, Belgium, Italy, and Poland.
