Types of Chocolate

There are different types of chocolate available, including dark, milk, and white chocolate. Each type varies based on the proportion of cocoa solids, milk, and sugar. Dark chocolate contains a higher percentage of cocoa, providing a rich, intense flavor, while milk chocolate is sweeter due to the addition of milk powder or condensed milk. White chocolate, on the other hand, is made primarily from cocoa butter, sugar, and milk, giving it a creamy and smooth texture.
